About me

I’m Saifeldin (Saif) Shehata, a bioinformatician with experience spanning CRO projects, academia, and industry. I’ve delivered 20+ multi‑omics analyses (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics), built and tested reproducible pipelines, and communicated results through clear, actionable reports and dashboards. I’m passionate about applying Generative AI to accelerate research—automating routine tasks, validating results with robust QA, and crafting transparent data stories.

Strengths

Reproducible workflows (Docker, Nextflow/Snakemake), CI/CD, versioning, results traceability, and audit‑ready documentation.

Data Skills

NGS processing, QC, association testing, functional analysis, biomarker discovery, text analytics, and automated reporting.

Communication

Client‑facing presentations, interactive notebooks/dashboards, and concise write‑ups that connect findings to decisions.

About the course

This 8‑week live cohort blends hands‑on workshops, help sessions, and a capstone showcase. The curriculum spans how LLMs work, document/web generation, AI‑assisted data analysis, literature/text mining, custom GPTs and chatbots, task automation, and research communication workflows.

Modules & themes

  • GenAI for research & text analysis

  • GenAI for communication & outreach
